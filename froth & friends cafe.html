Our Story

A Place Where Every Sip Feels Like Home

Froth and Friends is a cozy café in Vijayawada known for its warm ambience, delicious food, rich hot chocolate, handcrafted beverages, and perfect hangout vibe for friends and families.

Nestled in Maruti Cooperative Colony near Funtimes Club, we believe every great moment deserves great food and a comfortable seat. Whether it's a weekend brunch, a study session, or catching up with old friends — we're here to make it special.
